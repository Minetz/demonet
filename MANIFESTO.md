# The World's Take — Manifesto

## Every person has an opinion worth hearing

Not worth agreeing with. Worth *hearing*. The farmer worried about water. The student dreaming of a different country. The grandmother who has seen it all before. The dissident who cannot speak openly. Every one of them carries a perspective that the rest of us are poorer for not knowing.

We believe the great failure of the internet age is not that people lack a platform — it is that platforms lack the architecture for genuine understanding across distance, language, culture, and power.

## Our values

**Opinions are sacred. Identity is private.**
What you think matters. Who you are is yours alone. We separate the two absolutely. We strip personally identifiable information from every contribution while preserving the full substance and nuance of what was expressed. We never sell, trade, or weaponize the link between a person and their opinion.

**Transparency is not optional.**
Every system that touches your words is open. Every algorithm that clusters, translates, or surfaces opinions is auditable. If we cannot explain how something works, we do not ship it. Trust is built by showing your work, not by asking for faith.

**One human, one voice.**
The network is meaningless if a single actor can masquerade as a thousand. We use layered, probabilistic trust — behavioral patterns, device signals, participation history — to ensure that each voice in the network represents a real person. We do this without surveillance. Trust is visible; identity is not.

**AI assists. AI never manipulates.**
Artificial intelligence is our intermediary — it translates, it clusters, it finds bridges between people who would never otherwise encounter each other. It helps people express themselves more clearly. It never steers opinion. It never optimizes for engagement over understanding. It never decides what you should think.

**Bridges over bubbles.**
The default mode is discovery: surface the opinion you least expected from the person you least expected. Filter bubbles are an engineering choice, and we choose the opposite. Comfort is not a design goal. Understanding is.

**Open by default.**
The protocol is open. The data format is open. The API is open. We are not a platform to be locked into — we are infrastructure to be built upon. If someone can build a better interface to the world's opinions, they should, and they can.

**Reputation is earned and portable.**
Your history of participation — its consistency, its nuance, its good faith — follows you. Not as a score imposed by us, but as a cryptographic record you own and carry. Across platforms, across tools, across time. Your reputation belongs to you.

**We serve no master but clarity.**
No government, corporation, or ideology sets our agenda. The only question we optimize for is: *do people understand each other better than they did before?* If the answer is no, we have failed, regardless of growth, revenue, or prestige.

## What we are building

We are building the infrastructure for a global opinion network — starting with the simplest possible thing: one question, the whole planet answers, AI connects the dots. A place where you can sign your take, see it alongside the world's, and discover the closest mind in the most distant place. This is the seed. Everything else grows from here.
